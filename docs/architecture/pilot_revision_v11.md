# Development revision v11: operation evidence and applicability gate

Declared before implementation and measurement on 11 November 2026. Cost infrastructure
and frozen RQs/A-D remain as recorded. This phase tests operation applicability, validated
decision progress and observed work, not another cold/warm speedup or a trained selector.

## Primary denominator and probes

Keep all twelve existing generated mechanisms m01-m12 as the primary denominator.
Do not replace known difficult cases or add new research cases. Three repetitions of
five fixed probes produce 180 rows, in 36 query workers. Probe order rotates by query
and repetition. Each alternative starts from the same query-bound empty Checkpoint and
fresh function-entry project/state, with no imported semantic summaries or type inputs.

- bounded_paths: the unchanged eight-step symbolic check and fresh result validation.
- expanded_paths: the unchanged 128-step check and fresh validation.
- definitions_then_expanded: paid RDA/exact-prefix preparation, then expanded checking;
  preserve planning errors and pay for them even when checking falls back without reuse.
- direct_prefix_then_expanded: execute/cache the same eligible straight-line entry prefix
  without RDA, then expanded checking. This separates RDA from paid prefix execution.
- local_bounds: the corrected restricted byte-decoded checker and fresh certificate recheck.

Prefix preparation never skips instructions, has a 64-instruction limit, and publishes
snapshots only after exact single-successor execution reaches the declared target. Any
final property validation is fresh and reuses zero prefix steps. The original checker,
operation catalog and selector are not modified. A dependency graph, approximate slice
or prepared prefix is planning evidence, not a property decision.

## Records, costs and pre-action information

Each query worker has a 20 s parent process cap including startup, feature acquisition,
all probes, validation and recovery. Preserve all five identities on failure; late
decisive results become unknown with original evidence retained. Flush complete probe
records for recovery. Read gold only after all workers stop.

Measure required per-probe setup, preparation, execution and fresh validation, and retain
the complete parent worker charge separately. Common pre-action feature acquisition and
startup are not free deployment costs. Probe-local times are operation diagnostics;
never sum or amortize them into a claimed end-to-end policy speedup. Incomplete costs
and work counters remain explicitly incomplete, not zero-cost successes.

Before probing, record type-blind entry instruction count, target position in the entry
block, branch/call flags, immediate AND masks, argument ranges and capacity. Record a
fixed structural first-operation hint: local bounds for an observed immediate mask or
unsigned branch, otherwise bounded when the target lies within its cap, otherwise full.
This hint is not trained and its recorded first-choice outcome is not a deployed policy
measurement. A future policy must pay feature acquisition and selection costs and be
compared with fixed full checking and fixed local/full fallback.

Record validated decision progress separately from planning artifacts and observed
execution work. Keep symbolic executed/reused/validation steps separate from abstract
instruction counts; RDA graph size is not a decision utility. Compare bounded/full work
on shared correct answers, composite preparation/full cost and total executed work,
and empirical cheap/full cost ranges on stable correct answers. A nonoverlapping range
in three repeats is descriptive separation, not statistical significance.

## Separate compiled-input proof audit

The three existing compiled inputs are a separate post-hoc integration panel, not pooled
with the primary twelve or remeasured in the probe experiment. Genuine neural outputs
exist only for them. No missing neural candidates may be synthesized for m01-m12.

Inspect existing verified operand relations without using candidate labels/scores to
derive a result. For the supported identity/add-constant/mask forms, determine whether
the proof-backed base/index/address/length facts plus declared ranges already settle
the tiny first-write query. This is a deduction conditional on prior paid proof, not
new program verification or a free feature. Preserve original model predictions and
proofs, and do not expose these relations to primary pre-action features. If such facts
enter a later B/C/D experiment, include a facts-to-decision baseline and charge acquiring
and verifying them to every arm that uses them.

## Falsification and next action

Stop interpretation for any validated decision/reference-label disagreement. If no
alternative offers a reproducible applicability/cost tradeoff against full/fallback,
do not add a neural scheduler. If structural features already explain the useful choices,
record that limitation. Only a remaining choice warrants genuine-type information
ablation with the same scheduler; the present three mapped inputs cannot establish
generalization or train a benefit predictor. No inference, training, compilation,
download, installation, API use or rented hardware is required. All files stay on D.
